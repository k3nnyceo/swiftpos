Three things that quietly cost shops money

Most small shops still run on a notebook and memory. That's where money leaks.

Unrecorded debts

"I'll pay next week" turns into forgotten money. Record every credit sale with the customer's name and phone, and track part-payments until it's cleared.

Stock running out

Fast sellers go out of stock before you notice. Low-stock alerts show what to reorder before customers walk away.

Slow, messy sales

Scan a barcode with your phone camera or a USB scanner, tap Cash, Transfer, POS or Credit, and send the receipt on WhatsApp.

Questions

Is it free?

Waitlist members get free early access. We plan to keep a free plan; paid plans will cover cloud sync, staff accounts and reminders. Pricing will be shared with the waitlist first.

Does it need internet?

The prototype stores data on your device. The cloud version will back it up and sync it, and we are designing it to keep working when the network drops.

Will my customers' details be safe?

Customer names and phone numbers are only shown to the shop owner. We will never sell your data or message your customers without your say-so.

Can I try the prototype now?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you a link and walk you through it.
